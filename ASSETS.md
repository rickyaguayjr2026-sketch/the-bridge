# Third-party assets

Every non-code asset the app ships with (audio, images, fonts, etc.) is logged
here with its source and license, so the terms are on record rather than
relied on from memory. Add a new entry any time an asset is added.

## Video

### `app/src/main/res/raw/covenant_intro.mp4`
- **Content:** Covenant Intro cinematic sequence (John 1:1 -> paper/ember
  vortex into fire -> "Holy Holy Holy / Lord God Almighty / Worthy is the
  Lamb" with heart-and-rings motif), ~13s.
- **Generated with:** Grok Imagine (xAI), prompted and generated by Ricky.
- **Rights:** Per xAI's terms, the user owns Grok-generated outputs and may
  use them commercially; xAI provides no IP indemnification, so responsibility
  for any third-party rights issue in generated content rests with the user.
  No such issue is expected here (generic cinematic/text visuals from an
  original prompt).
- **Modification:** Original file included baked-in audio/music; that audio
  was stripped (`ffmpeg -an`) and replaced by muxing in
  `covenant_swell.mp3` (see below) as the single audio track. First version
  (2026-07-04) trimmed the audio to the video's ~10s length, which felt
  abrupt; fixed (2026-07-06) by holding the video's last frame for the
  remaining ~3s (`tpad`) instead, so the full ~13s track plays out over a
  frozen final frame rather than getting cut off.
- **Added:** 2026-07-04, audio-cutoff fix 2026-07-06

### Armor of God plates — all 6, `app/src/main/res/drawable/armor_*.jpg`
Watercolor-and-ink illustrations of the six pieces of the Armor of God
(Ephesians 6:14-17), each on a cream parchment ground, matching the
hand-drawn "Bible Project" style used elsewhere. Prompted and generated by
Ricky, 2026-09-05/06. Added 2026-09-06, used in the Sanctuary ceremony.

- **Generated with:** Google Flow (Google) — NOT Grok Imagine. This is a
  different provider from every other generated asset in this file, so the
  rights basis documented for the Grok assets above does not carry over.
- **Rights — OPEN, NOT VERIFIED.** Google's generative-AI terms have not been
  reviewed for this project. Do not assume they match xAI's (user owns output,
  no indemnification); they are a separate document and may differ on
  ownership, commercial use, and attribution. **This must be resolved before
  release**, not before merge. Two things to check specifically:
  1. What Google's applicable terms say about commercial use of Flow output.
  2. Whether outputs carry SynthID or similar provenance watermarking, and
     whether that imposes any disclosure obligation.

**Quality caveat — not uniform.** Belt, shield, helmet and sword are true
illustrations at 1376x768. Breastplate and shoes are *saved video frames* at
640x360 and upscale roughly 1.5-2x at render width, so they read visibly
softer than the other four. They were wired in knowingly as an improvement
over the old bordered placeholder box, not as final art. Re-rendering them
the way the other four were made is a drop-in file replacement at the same
resource names — no code change required.

**Still missing:** the seventh asset — the fully-armored closing composite for
`ArmoredClosing`. Either the six pieces composited onto one base figure, or a
single illustration of all pieces worn together. Until it exists, the closing
beat of the ceremony still renders a labeled placeholder.

**Bundled size:** ~2.6MB for the six as committed. Re-rendering the two soft
pieces at full resolution would put the set near ~3.7MB, and ~4.3MB once the
composite lands.
